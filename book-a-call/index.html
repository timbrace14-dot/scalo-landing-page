My mission: barbers who cut because they want to, not because they have to

SCALO started in January 2025, on a trip to Thailand with my mates.

I'd been cutting for ten years by then. Good column, decent money, business I was proud of. And sat out there I worked out that the money only existed if I was stood behind the chair.

So I built something else out of what I already knew. I still cut, about eight days a month, twelve if I want them. I just don't have to. I like it more now than I did when it was the only way I earned.

Ten years behind the chair.

I'm not here to talk anyone out of barbering. I still do it.

What I want is for you to have the option. Because the minute cutting is the only way you can earn, you start resenting it. That's what ruins a trade you were once excited about.

A barber should be able to:

  • Cut because he wants to, not because he has to
  • Earn from what he knows, not just what he does
  • Charge more for the chair time he does keep
  • Be seen as someone who runs a business, not just someone who's handy with clippers

I built mine out of what was already in my head. Ten years of it, sat there, never sold.

FAQ

Does this work for my industry?
Yes. SCALO is built for service based founders across industries, not one niche. Packaging your knowledge into an online offer and building the engine behind it works the same wherever your skill sits.
I already run a business, where is the time?
The whole programme installs around a full schedule. A few focused hours a week is enough. Your portal and the pre built frameworks mean you are never starting from a blank page, and the weekly calls keep you moving.
What exactly do I get on day one?
Within 24 hours of joining, your portal is live with every framework, template and script, a 120 day roadmap personalised to you, over 10 hours of recorded content, WhatsApp access to me and the team, and an onboarding call to lock your plan.
Do I need a big audience already?
You need a skill people value and a small audience to speak to. Most founders come in with a few thousand followers and momentum, not a million. We build the system that turns that attention into income.
Do you just hand me videos, or actually help?
This is hands on. Weekly group coaching, one to one calls at the key moments, and WhatsApp support with a 24 hour response for the full 120 days. The content is there to support the work, not replace the guidance.
Is my first sale guaranteed?
No, and anyone promising that is lying to you. What I guarantee is the system, the frameworks, and my time. What you get out of it depends on you doing the work. The founders who apply everything tend to see their first sale inside the first 45 days.

We cover that on the call. Watch the training, book a slot, and we will walk through the investment, the options, and whether SCALO is the right fit for where you are right now. No pressure either way.
