Yes. SCALO is built for service based founders across industries, not one niche. Packaging your knowledge into an online offer and building the engine behind it works the same wherever your expertise sits.

The whole programme installs around a full schedule. A few focused hours a week is enough. Your portal and the pre built frameworks mean you are never starting from a blank page, and the weekly calls keep you moving.

This is hands on. Weekly group coaching, one to one calls at the key moments, and WhatsApp support with a 24 hour response for the full 120 days. The content is there to support the work, not replace the guidance.

No, and anyone promising that is lying to you. What I guarantee is the system, the frameworks, and my time. What you get out of it depends on you doing the work. The founders who apply everything tend to see their first sale inside the first 45 days.
