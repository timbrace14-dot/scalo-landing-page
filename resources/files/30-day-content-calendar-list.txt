FILMING DAY PLAN

Session one, about 90 minutes, shop closed.
The 12 opinion and education videos: days 2, 4, 7, 10, 12, 15, 17, 20, 22, 25, 27, 30.
  - Phone at eye height on the station, window to one side, never the ceiling light alone.
  - One take each. Say the hook, speak 20 to 40 seconds, stop.
  - Change your top every four videos.
  - Say the day number at the start of each take, trim it off later.

Session two, across two normal working days.
The 18 transformations, client stories and behind the scenes clips.
  - Film the before of every client, three clips during, the turn at the end.
  - Ask the client first, every time.
  - Choose at the end of the day, not while you are filming.
